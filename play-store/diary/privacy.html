English

DIARY (ge.weeklyplus.app) is an offline personal planner. No account is needed. The app has no ads or analytics trackers and does not transmit tasks, goals, or personal information to developer servers.

Your tasks, plans, dates, goals, completion records and settings are stored locally on your Android device. The app does not provide cloud synchronization.

The app may request Android notification permission for local reminders and uses the device boot event to restore scheduled reminders. Reminder data is not sent to the developer.

Exporting or importing a JSON backup only happens at your request. You choose where that file is stored or shared. To delete your locally stored information, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app.
