# PIRALI Beta 1.44 — Combat and pickups

- Health when already at maximum, Mauser bullets at 10/10, the one-hit laurel armour shield when active, and shotgun shells at 16/16 are left on the ground. They are collected only after usable space becomes available. No double-award of pickups.
- Primary generals, both stage-8 generals, Devil and the stage-11 wave-4/5 generals gain 20% health. Phase thresholds scale to retain their relative timing.
- Boss tactics now lead Simona's movement and use paced flank movements; Devil additionally pursues 15% faster and shoots moderately more often, keeping existing telegraphs and bullet damage.
- Letters and all other side objectives complete when approached; no manual interaction button or keypress.
- At the stage-2 captive objective a drawn villager replaces the letter and visibly runs away after being rescued. Story progression and cosmetics are preserved.
- A collected one-hit shield is displayed as a brilliant yellow upper semicircle instead of the previous green full ring.
- Player and Developer web versions share all mechanics; old scoring, Firebase, joystick, 11 stages and the 5000+2000 point cap remain unchanged.

QA: node behaviour tests and earlier Beta 1.31–1.43 regression checks, real mobile Chromium Player/Developer tests and GitHub Pages publication. Google Play APK/AAB unchanged.
