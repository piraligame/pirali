# PIRALI Beta 1.40 — Portrait-First Menu and Stealth Removal

The existing compact main menu is lowered using available viewport height and measured panel height to reveal Simona's face while keeping the bottom four-language selector in view. All title/collection/community entries remain unchanged.

Stealth is completely removed: no sneak button, no slowed movement or hiding modifier to detection, no dedicated tall-grass patches or stealth-related shooting hooks. The exact Beta 1.38 joystick and original movement speed are restored and preserved; Beta 1.37 sight ranges are unchanged.

The lookout alarm, commander, ambushes, 11 extra missions, historical-fiction story entries, cosmetic cloaks, all 11 stages and fifth-wave mode remain intact. Score budgets (5000+2000) and the Full Route 20% points/seven hearts rules remain intact. Developer comparison remains for other Evolution mechanics, not stealth (always removed).

QA tests mobile portrait menu visibility in all four languages, absence of stealth, and all prior Beta 1.31–1.39 mechanics. Web preview only; no Android APK/AAB or Play Console upload.
