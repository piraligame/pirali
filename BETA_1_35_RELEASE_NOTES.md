# PIRALI Beta 1.35 — cinematic Simona menu

Implemented from the selected 30 October 2026 visual reference, without replacing the playable background with a screenshot.

- Original illustrated Simona background remains the real menu image. The layout sits lower to show Simona's hooded face above the card.
- Exact original opening story **in full** floats on the upper-right sky, outside the menu card, in the four already-supported game languages. No invented or truncated Georgian prose.
- The language selector moves to **below the full menu actions and player name**, above privacy and version.
- The actual saved player name appears **directly after My Statistics**, not on the bottom outside the action stack. Name validation and progress keys are unchanged.
- Modern gold/black translucent menu; compact responsive mobile buttons, with scrolling when a small viewport cannot fit both portrait and menu.
- Georgian Gamers Guild logo/link, map, earned Full Route, leaderboard, achievements, checkpoint, joysticks, gameplay and Developer tools are preserved.
- Both Player and Developer are independently QA tested.

Web preview only. No APK/AAB built or uploaded to Google Play.
